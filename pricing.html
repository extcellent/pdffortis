Questions, answered

Everything people usually ask before switching.
Is it true my files never leave my device?
Yes, on every plan — Free, Pro, and Team. Merging, splitting, compressing, signing, and translating all run locally in your browser, so nothing is uploaded to process it.
What happens when I hit the Free plan's daily limit?
You can keep using the tools, but downloads pause until the next day — 3 a day if you're signed in, 1 a day if you're not. Pro and Team remove that limit entirely.
Does PDF translation cost extra on paid plans?
No. Translation is included in Pro and Team at no extra cost, with no daily cap — unlike the Free plan's 1-per-day limit.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly billing?
Yes — toggle between them any time using the switch at the top of this page. Yearly billing is 20% cheaper on both Pro and Team.
What's included in the Team plan beyond Pro?
Team covers everything in Pro for up to 5 seats, plus team chat, an activity feed, 48-hour shared links, an admin dashboard, and a history view showing who changed what. It also starts with a 30-day free trial.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There's no lock-in on any paid plan — cancel whenever you like, no hidden fees either way.
