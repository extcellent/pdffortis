Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026
Privacy-First Guarantee: Your documents are processed locally on your device. We do not upload, read, or store your private PDF files during standard single-user operations.

1. Overview & Data Controller

PDFortis is designed around a client-side architecture to protect your documents. For any privacy inquiries or account management, contact us at:

Data Controller Contact
pdfortis@proton.me

2. Local Browser Processing

When using our online PDF tools (merging, editing, compressing, translating, or signing), processing takes place entirely within your browser environment:

  • Zero Server Uploads: Files remain in local RAM and are not transferred to backend servers.
  • Embedded Metadata & History: Document revision history and signature states are embedded directly into the generated PDF binary or local storage on your device.
  • Browser Translations: Document translation tasks execute locally via client-side scripts.

3. Team Workspace & Temporary Storage

If you create an account and use team collaboration features, limited cloud processing applies strictly to shared assets:

  • Explicit PDF Sharing: Documents are uploaded to Supabase Storage only when you explicitly select "Share with Team." Shared link access defaults to a strict 48-hour expiration window.
  • Automatic File Purging: Shared files and associated activity entries are automatically purged from servers upon expiration.
  • Team Messaging: Team chat messages are stored in Supabase and automatically removed after 7 days.

4. Usage Tracking & Abuse Prevention

To enforce free usage quotas without requiring cookies or user registration, we use anonymized technical identifiers:

Anonymous Fingerprinting
We generate a one-way SHA-256 hash derived from your browser headers (screen resolution, language, user-agent, timezone). This hash cannot be reversed into personally identifiable information and is automatically deleted from download logs after 8 hours.

5. Infrastructure & Subprocessors

We rely on trusted third-party cloud infrastructure to serve web assets and handle optional team workspace databases:

  • Vercel Inc.: Hosting platform for static application assets.
  • Supabase Inc.: Cloud database and storage provider for team authentication, active subscriptions, and temporary shared links.

6. Your Rights under GDPR / DSGVO

You have full ownership of your data. Registered workspace users retain the right to:

  • Request access to stored profile information.
  • Update or correct account details via the Dashboard.
  • Delete your account and all linked workspace profiles instantly using the account deletion workflow.
